Throve Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

1. About this policy

Throve (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the mobile application “Throve” (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

Account information: If you use the App, we may process your email and profile details.

Profile and content: We may process information you add to your profile and listings.

Messages and offers: If you use messaging features, we process message content to enable communication.

Orders (prototype): This prototype uses simulated checkout and does not collect or process real payment card/bank information.

Reviews and ratings: If you submit reviews/ratings, we process what you provide.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and maintain account features
  • Enable browsing, seller profiles, offers, messaging, and order simulations
  • Enable live shopping sessions (where applicable)
  • Enable review/rating submission after completed orders

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the App and provide its features.

This prototype does not process real payments.

5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the App, maintain account functionality, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

6. Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@throve.app
