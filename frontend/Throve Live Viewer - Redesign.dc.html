Device safety
Overlays sit inside 14px side insets and above a 22px bottom safe area, so nothing lands under a notch or gesture bar. On short screens the chat stack drops to two comments before anything else shrinks. Both sheets cap at 76% of viewport height and scroll internally, with Buy now and Add to cart pinned outside the scroll area. When the keyboard opens, the sheets close and only the input row lifts.
