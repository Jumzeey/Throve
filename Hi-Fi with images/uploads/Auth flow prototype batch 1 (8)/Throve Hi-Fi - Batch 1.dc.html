Welcome & accounts

Screens 1–5, restyled from the approved low-fidelity structure using the Classical system: Cormorant Garamond over Lora, a single warm-gold accent applied as stroke rather than fill, hairline dividers, and matted photography. Nothing about the account journeys, fields, copy meaning or navigation changed — only presentation.

Buttons stay outlined per the system's rule (no filled CTAs); weight and the accent line carry emphasis instead of a solid fill. A second accent — a warm coral — is reserved for the Live tab and live badges in later batches; it doesn't appear in these five auth screens.

Frames below are shown at mobile width (328px) with a hairline device edge, not a literal bezel — the system's editorial language reads better as a matted card than a glossy phone mockup.
